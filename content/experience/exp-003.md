Developed LSTMSE-Net, a novel audio-visual speech enhancement model that isolates and enhances speaker audio in noisy environments. Engineered a temporal feature extraction pipeline using RNN and LSTM units to jointly model audio-visual dependencies. The model achieved a 3× reduction in inference time compared to the baseline while improving speech quality. The original paper was accepted at InterspeechW 2024. Currently working on an advanced version using a ConvNeXtV2-based video pipeline and an audio decoder inspired by deep state space modeling.
